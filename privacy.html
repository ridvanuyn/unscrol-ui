Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

The full and current Unscrol Privacy Policy is published at https://unscrol.com/privacy/. This page redirects there. If it does not, please follow the link. The summary below is only a short overview; the policy at unscrol.com is the authoritative text.

Summary

  • Account data — a guest, email or Google account on our servers: optional email address, a hashed PIN, display name, and the check-ins, mood entries, streaks, focus sessions and leaderboard scores you create.
  • Screen Time and Usage Access stay on your device — on iPhone, Apple's Screen Time, Family Controls and Device Activity data is never sent to us; on Android, Usage Access and "display over other apps" are used on-device only. Blocked-app selections stay on your device.
  • Analytics and diagnostics — Mixpanel and Firebase Analytics (usage events, which may be linked to your Unscrol user identifier) and Sentry (crash and performance reports, linked to your user identifier but not your email or name). These services may derive an approximate location from your IP address; Unscrol does not request GPS or precise location.
  • Subscriptions — RevenueCat, on top of the App Store and Google Play. We never receive your payment details.
  • Advertising measurement — the Meta (Facebook) SDK receives advertising identifiers, device information and app events such as install, registration, trial and subscription. We do not send your Unscrol account identifier to Meta.
  • No sale of personal data — we do not sell, trade or rent your personal information. Your display name and score are visible on the public leaderboard.
  • Deleting your data — in the app, go to Profile and tap Delete Account. Instructions, including what third-party providers keep, are at unscrol.com/delete-account.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@unscrol.com
